## What you just used

- **A query** asks for the task information a view needs. Filtering reuses what is already known.
- **A command** asks to complete a task. Its **action** keeps track of progress and the result.
- **An edit session** keeps your draft and the saved version it came from together.

These are the [four concepts](/docs/data/start/concepts) used throughout Vem. You do not need to manage requests or subscriptions in a feature component.

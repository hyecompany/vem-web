## Behavior and choices

SheetTrigger opens SheetContent; SheetClose dismisses it. Keep a SheetTitle and, when useful, SheetDescription inside the content. The dialog handles focus, Escape, and returning focus to the trigger.

Use `side` on SheetContent to select the edge. Use controlled `open` and `onOpenChange` when another part of the application owns visibility. Keep forms and their state in your application; dismissing a sheet does not save a draft.
